# Jeremiah Campbell | Music Ed Portfolio

## What's in this folder
- `index.html`: the whole site. Every section has a comment explaining how to fill it in.
- `style.css`: colors and fonts. Change the colors at the top and they update everywhere.
- `images/`: your photos.
- `files/`: put PDFs here (CV, lesson plans).

## Put it online for free with GitHub Pages
1. Make a free account at github.com.
2. Create a new **public** repository named `yourusername.github.io` (use your actual username).
3. Click **uploading an existing file** and drag in everything from this folder: `index.html`, `style.css`, `README.md`, and the `images` and `files` folders.
4. Click **Commit changes**.
5. Within a minute or two your site is live at `https://yourusername.github.io`.

To update the site later, open the file on github.com, click the pencil icon, edit it, and commit. To add a PDF or photo, go to the `files` or `images` folder and choose **Add file > Upload files**.

## Adding things over time
- **Mission statement:** replace the text in the `<blockquote>` near the top and delete `class="placeholder"`.
- **Videos:** upload to YouTube as *Unlisted*, then click Share > Embed and paste the code where the comments say.
- **Lesson plans and CV:** upload the PDF to `files/` and follow the comment in that section.
- **Each MuEd course:** copy the example `<li>` in the Coursework section.
