About me

I'm a guitarist, teacher, and Music Education student at Portland State University, where jazz guitar is my principal instrument. I've taught guitar, piano, and ukulele to students of all ages since 2019. Right now I teach kids after school through the Ukulele Project.

Outside the classroom I'm a music producer. I've helped artists record and mix their albums, and I run sound for live events. I also write songs and poetry.
